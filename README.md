# ⚡ Dopamine Recovery Lab

## Interactive Recovery Neuroscience

Dopamine Recovery Lab is an interactive psychoeducation tool designed to help patients understand how reward, motivation, cravings, boredom, stress, and recovery affect the brain’s dopamine system.

This project blends:

* Recovery neuroscience
* Trauma-informed psychoeducation
* Emotional regulation concepts
* Behavioral activation
* Patient-friendly brain education
* Interactive learning tools

The app is designed to reduce shame, increase insight, and help patients better understand why recovery can initially feel emotionally flat, boring, exhausting, or difficult.

---

# 🌱 Core Therapeutic Message

> “The brain often rebuilds motivation after action, not before it.”

And:

> “Healing the reward system is usually less about intensity and more about repetition.”

This project helps patients understand:

* Why cravings feel urgent
* Why boredom can feel overwhelming
* Why healthy activities may initially feel unrewarding
* How stress affects reward systems
* Why quick dopamine can become addictive
* How the brain slowly relearns pleasure and motivation during recovery

---

# 🎯 Features

## 📈 Dopamine Loop Visualizer

Interactive psychoeducation explaining the cycle of:

Stress / Emptiness → Quick Relief → Dopamine Spike → Crash → Lower Baseline → More Cravings

The goal is to help patients understand:

* Why short-term relief can increase long-term distress
* How high-intensity stimulation affects reward sensitivity
* Why the brain may begin chasing relief harder while enjoying life less

---

## 🎚️ Reward Sensitivity Meter

A visual demonstration of how repeated high-intensity stimulation may temporarily lower sensitivity to everyday rewards.

Topics include:

* Reward recalibration
* Nervous system adaptation
* Dopamine sensitivity
* Recovery boredom
* Emotional flatness
* Anhedonia

# 🛠️ Recovery Reward Plan Builder

Patients can build a personalized recovery reward plan using healthy dopamine-supporting activities.

Each selected activity includes:

* Approximate dose/time recommendations
* Measurable units
* Brain and nervous system benefits
* Why the activity may help
* Small “Try This” action steps

Current activities include:

* Exercise
* Music
* Sunlight
* Journaling
* Connection
* Creative hobbies
* Walking
* Helping others
* Meditation
* Healthy meals
* Cold water / sauna
* Sleep routine

# 💬 Suggested Group Questions

* What does your brain chase when stressed, lonely, or exhausted?
