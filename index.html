⚖️ Fast Dopamine vs Slow Dopamine

Fast dopamine often creates intensity quickly. Slow dopamine usually builds more gradually but tends to be more stable and sustainable.

⚡ Fast Dopamine

  • Substances
  • Scrolling
  • Gambling
  • Chaos & drama
  • Impulsive spending
  • Risky behavior
  • Instant gratification

🌱 Slow Dopamine

  • Exercise
  • Music
  • Connection
  • Creativity
  • Sunlight & movement
  • Accomplishment
  • Helping others
Recovery often means retraining the brain to notice slower forms of reward again.
